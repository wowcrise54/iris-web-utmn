## Variant: Calm Editorial

### Design stance
Clean, readable interface focused on content clarity with subtle modern touches and a professional, calm aesthetic.

### Key choices
- **Layout**: Spacious grid with generous whitespace
- **Typography**: Light font weights, readable sizes, clear hierarchy
- **Color**: Pastel status indicators on light background, minimal contrast
- **Interaction**: Subtle hover effects, smooth transitions
- **Shadows**: Minimal, soft shadows for depth

### Trade-offs
- **Strong at**: Readability, long-term viewing comfort, professional appearance
- **Weak at**: Information density, quick status scanning in low-light environments

### Best for
- Security teams who prefer a calm, professional interface
- Daytime operations with good ambient lighting
- Users who prioritize readability over information density
- Organizations with a conservative brand aesthetic

### Comparison
- More readable than utilitarian dense for extended periods
- Less "futuristic" than glassmorphism variant
- Best balance of modern aesthetics and practical usability
